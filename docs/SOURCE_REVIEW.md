# SVCE source review checklist

## Known starting points
- https://svce.edu.in/
- https://svce.edu.in/admission.php
- https://svce.edu.in/quicklinks.php
- https://svce.edu.in/aboutus.php
- https://svce.edu.in/contactus.php
- https://svce.edu.in/aicte.php

## Source review rules
- Confirm that each source is for **SVCE Tirupati**, not a similarly named institution or another campus.
- For current dates, fees, examination schedules, admission eligibility, and policies, record the relevant academic year or publication date.
- Keep official sources separate from external explanatory sources.
- Prefer current official notices over undated summary pages when they directly conflict, but flag unresolved conflicts for human review.
- Preserve document URL, title, retrieval date, and extraction status.
- A successful crawl does not prove factual correctness or completeness.
- Some PDFs may be scanned and need OCR; some dynamic pages may need browser-based extraction.
- Check robots.txt, terms of use, and permission requirements before broad crawling.
