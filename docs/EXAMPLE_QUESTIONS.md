# Starter evaluation questions

These are test prompts, not claims that the answer is already present in the index.

1. What is the official website for SVCE Tirupati?
2. Where can I find the admissions process?
3. Which undergraduate and postgraduate programmes are listed?
4. Where are academic regulations and syllabi published?
5. How can I contact the principal's office?
6. Where can I find AICTE approval documents?
7. Where can I find department and faculty information?
8. Where are placement resources published?
9. What is the latest official examination notification?
10. What are the current admission deadlines?
11. What are the current tuition and other fee amounts?
12. Where can I find hostel and transport details?
13. What is the attendance requirement in the applicable regulation?
14. How do I apply for a bonafide certificate?
15. What scholarships are currently available?
16. Can you summarize a specific academic regulation?
17. Which source confirms this answer?
18. I found two different dates; which source is current?
19. Is this notice for the current academic year?
20. If the website does not publish an answer, can you tell me where to verify it?
